# Purvabhas: Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization

Inventory mismanagement costs local businesses thousands of dollars. Purvabhas solves this by accurately predicting future demand using historical sales data, promotions, and external factors like holidays.

## Architecture & Modeling
- **Algorithm:** XGBoost Regressor combined with statistical time-series forecasting.
- **Features:** Lagged variables, rolling windows, and categorical embeddings for product IDs.
- **Optimization:** Calculated dynamic reorder points and safety stock levels based on predicted demand variance and lead times.

## Business Impact
- **Reduced Overstock:** Prevented dead inventory by optimizing safety stock margins.
- **Improved Availability:** Achieved a 98% in-stock rate for high-velocity items.
- **Cost Savings:** Lowered warehousing and carrying costs significantly.
